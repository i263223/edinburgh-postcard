Scottish Food to Try

Dish / Drink What It Is
Haggis, Neeps & Tatties Scotland's national dish served with mashed turnips and potatoes.
Cullen Skink A rich, creamy soup made with smoked haddock, potatoes, and onions.
Cranachan A traditional dessert made with whipped cream, honey, raspberries, and toasted oats.
Irn-Bru Scotland's iconic bright orange fizzy soft drink.

Essential Travel Tips

1. Pack for Four Seasons in One Day

Edinburgh weather changes rapidly—sunshine, wind, and rain can all happen within an hour. Always carry a waterproof jacket and wear layers.

2. Wear Comfortable Shoes

The Old Town is full of steep hills, narrow closes (alleyways), and uneven cobblestone streets. Leave high heels or thin-soled shoes behind.

3. Use Lothian Buses & Trams

Edinburgh is very walkable, but for longer trips, tap your contactless card on any Lothian Bus. The system automatically caps your daily fare.
