Places to Visit in Edinburgh

1. Edinburgh Castle

Perched on Castle Rock, this historic fortress dominates the skyline of Edinburgh. Home to the Scottish Crown Jewels and the Stone of Destiny, it offers breathtaking panoramic views of the city.

2. Dean Village

A peaceful, picturesque oasis located just a 15-minute walk from Princes Street. Once a thriving grain-milling village along the Water of Leith, it is famous for its cobblestone streets and 19th-century architecture.

3. The Meadows in Bruntsfield

A large, vibrant public park just south of the city centre. It is the favorite local spot for sunny afternoon picnics, cherry blossom walks in spring, and outdoor games.
